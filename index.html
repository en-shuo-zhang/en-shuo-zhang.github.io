Research

Deep-learning receiver for underwater acoustic OTFS

Master's thesis · Intelligent IoT Laboratory, NTOU · 2024 – present

In progress Designing a hybrid CNN-Transformer receiver that detects OTFS symbols directly from the delay-Doppler grid, for underwater channels with strong Doppler and multipath. Done so far: a baseline OTFS link simulated on a public benchmark of measured underwater channels, verified in Python and MATLAB. Next: the learned receiver, then tests across five sites from lab tanks to a 50 m pool.
